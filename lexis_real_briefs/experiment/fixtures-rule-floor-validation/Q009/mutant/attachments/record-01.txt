Exhibit A: Affidavit of Kizzy Owens
Kizzy Owens states under oath that she paid her $200 monthly tenant share on the first of each month. Arborcrest Properties served her a termination notice dated March 10, 2022. The PHA made the March 2022 HAP payment of $800 on April 15, 2022. The lease began on January 1, 2022, and was set to expire on December 31, 2022. The rent was $1,000 per month, with Owens responsible for $200 and the PHA responsible for $800.
