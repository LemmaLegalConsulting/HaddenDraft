Kizzy Owens, Tenant-Appellee

Statement of Facts
The HCV lease between Kizzy Owens and Arborcrest Properties ran from January 1, 2022, to December 31, 2022. The HAP contract was coterminous with the lease. Owens paid her $200 monthly tenant share on time. Arborcrest Properties served Owens a termination notice dated March 10, 2022. The PHA's HAP share was $800 monthly.

Argument
Arborcrest Properties' termination notice cannot stand because it violates 24 C.F.R. § 982.310(b). The regulation prohibits terminating the lease during the HAP contract term except for serious or repeated lease violations, good cause, or other serious or repeated violations of the HAP contract.

The termination notice rested on the $800 shortfall in PHA assistance for March 2022, which was paid late on April 15, 2022, after the termination notice was issued on March 10, 2022.

The tenant's payment of the tenant share is not in dispute. The lease and HAP contract expressly bifurcate rent into tenant and PHA shares.

Conclusion
The court should rule in favor of Kizzy Owens and deny Arborcrest Properties' nonpayment eviction claim.
