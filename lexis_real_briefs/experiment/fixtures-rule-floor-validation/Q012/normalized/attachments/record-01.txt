Exhibit A: CMHA Grievance Policy

Section I: Grievance Procedure
1. A tenant may request a grievance hearing within ten (10) business days of receiving a notice of termination.
2. The hearing will be scheduled within thirty (30) days of the request.
3. The tenant will be notified in writing of the hearing date.

Section II: Hearing Scheduling
1. The hearing panel will be composed of three members: one selected by the tenant, one selected by CMHA, and one neutral third party.
2. The hearing will be conducted in a fair and impartial manner.
