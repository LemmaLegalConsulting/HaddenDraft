IN THE CUYAHOGA COUNTY COURT OF COMMON PLEAS

HARRISON,
Plaintiff,
vs.
CLEVELAND METROPOLITAN HOUSING AUTHORITY,
Defendant.

MEMORANDUM IN SUPPORT OF TENANT'S MOTION FOR PRELIMINARY INJUNCTION

Statement of Facts

Cleveland Metropolitan Housing Authority (CMHA) operates Riverview Estates public housing. On February 10, 2023, CMHA served Harrison a termination notice effective April 11, 2023, citing lease violation for unauthorized guests. Harrison timely requested a grievance hearing on February 22, 2023.

Argument

Under 24 C.F.R. § 966.4(l)(3), a PHA-operated public housing tenancy can be terminated only in accordance with § 966.4(l). CMHA operates Riverview Estates (§ 966.4(l)(1)). The termination notice stated the ground as 'unauthorized guest' and was served on February 10, 2023, with a termination date of April 11, 2023 (§ 966.4(l)(3)(i)). The notice quoted the lease clause and stated Harrison's right to reply and inspect documents (§ 966.4(l)(3)(ii)). The notice informed Harrison of the right to a grievance hearing (§ 966.51-.57). Harrison timely requested a hearing.

The grievance hearing was scheduled for May 5, 2023, after the April 11, 2023 termination date, showing that a timely requested, available grievance process was not completed before termination.

As per 24 C.F.R. § 966.57, a PHA must provide a grievance process. CMHA's grievance procedure is available (CMHA Grievance Policy, Ex. A). Harrison requested a hearing within the timeframe specified by CMHA's policy. CMHA claims no exclusion applies here.

Conclusion

The court should rule that CMHA's termination of Harrison's tenancy violated 24 C.F.R. §§ 966.4(l)(3), 966.51-.57, and grant Harrison's motion for preliminary injunction.
