Cedarbrook Housing terminated tenant Shenequa Bryant's lease and filed this eviction action. Shenequa Bryant opposes, arguing that Cedarbrook Housing failed to comply with HUD Handbook 4350.3 REV-1 ¶ 8-13.B.

Statement of Facts
Shenequa Bryant resides at Cedarbrook Housing, a project-based Section 8 assisted property. On February 10, 2023, Cedarbrook Housing served Bryant with a notice of termination stating that she had 10 days to request a discussion. The notice quoted HUD Handbook 4350.3 REV-1 ¶ 8-13.B and stated: 'You have the right to discuss this termination with management within 10 days of receiving this notice.' On February 14, 2023, Bryant timely requested a discussion by email. On February 24, 2023, Cedarbrook Housing filed this eviction action.

Argument
Cedarbrook Housing's termination notice complied with HUD Handbook 4350.3 REV-1 ¶ 8-13.B by quoting the advisement. The project-based assistance is governed by this handbook provision.

The handbook requires that the owner honor a timely request for discussion. Bryant's request was made within the 10-day period.

Conclusion
The court should rule in favor of Shenequa Bryant because Cedarbrook Housing did not comply with HUD Handbook 4350.3 REV-1 ¶ 8-13.B.
