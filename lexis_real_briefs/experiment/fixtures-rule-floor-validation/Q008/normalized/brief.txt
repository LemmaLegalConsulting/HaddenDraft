In the Court of Common Pleas of Franklin County, Ohio

Jasmine Patel's Motion to Dismiss Eviction Action

Statement of Facts
Jasmine Patel is a tenant in the Housing Choice Voucher program administered by the Columbus Metropolitan Housing Authority (CMHA). On February 10, 2023, the landlord, Greenwood Properties, served Patel with a notice to vacate. The landlord filed this eviction action on February 22, 2023.

Argument
The regulation at 24 C.F.R. § 982.310(e)(2)(ii) requires that the owner give the PHA a copy of any notice to vacate at the same time it is served on the tenant.

The evidence shows that the owner served Patel with the notice on February 10, 2023, but did not send the same notice to CMHA until four days later, on February 14, 2023.

Conclusion
The court should dismiss this eviction action because the owner failed to comply with 24 C.F.R. § 982.310(e)(2)(ii).
