IN THE COURT OF COMMON PLEAS OF CUYAHOGA COUNTY, OHIO

JENNINGS PROPERTY MANAGEMENT,
Plaintiff,
vs.
KATRINA WASHINGTON,
Defendant.

DEFENDANT'S MEMORANDUM IN SUPPORT OF JUDGMENT

Statement of Facts

On February 10, 2023, Plaintiff Jennings Property Management served Defendant Katrina Washington with a Three-Day Notice for nonpayment of rent. Defendant remained in possession and, on February 17, 2023, Plaintiff filed this action. On March 3, 2023, the Court ordered Defendant to deposit $1,200.00 into the Court's depository by March 10, 2023, and continue making monthly deposits of $1,200.00 by the first day of each month thereafter. Defendant complied with the order. The parties stipulated to Defendant's counterclaim under R.C. Chapter 5321 in the amount of $2,500.00.

Argument

Pursuant to R.C. 1923.04 and R.C. 1923.061(A), Plaintiff has brought this action and Defendant has counterclaimed. R.C. 1923.061(B) governs the effect of a counterclaim judgment on the alleged rent due.

Calculations show that after applying the $2,500.00 counterclaim judgment and the $1,200.00 initial deposit, plus $1,200.00 deposited on April 1, 2023, totaling $4,900.00, against the alleged $3,600.00 rent due, no rent remains due after counterclaim judgment and credited deposits.

R.C. 1923.061(B) requires the court to consider the counterclaim judgment and credited deposits when determining the amount of rent due.

Conclusion

The Court should rule in favor of Defendant and dismiss this action.
