Plaintiff, Brynn Kiley, respectfully submits this memorandum in support of her claim against Defendant, Harrison Properties, for damages related to her security deposit.

Statement of Facts
Brynn Kiley paid a security deposit of $850 on January 5, 2022, for a rental property managed by Harrison Properties. Her tenancy ended on July 31, 2022, when she returned the property. Harrison Properties failed to return or itemize the security deposit within thirty days.

Argument
R.C. 5321.16 governs the return of security deposits in Ohio. Under division (B), a landlord must return the security deposit or provide an itemized list of deductions within thirty days after the tenant vacates the premises. If the landlord fails to comply, the tenant is entitled to double the amount of the security deposit under division (C).

The security deposit was $850, paid on January 5, 2022. The tenancy ended on July 31, 2022. Harrison Properties failed to return or itemize the deposit by August 30, 2022, thirty days later.

On August 10, 2022, Brynn Kiley provided Harrison Properties with her written forwarding address via certified mail, return receipt requested, which was received on August 12, 2022.

Thus, under R.C. 5321.16(C), Brynn Kiley is entitled to double the security deposit amount, totaling $1,700. She also seeks attorney fees as permitted by the statute. Notably, she does not claim statutory interest on the deposit.

Conclusion
The court should rule in favor of Brynn Kiley, awarding her $1,700 in damages and reasonable attorney fees.
