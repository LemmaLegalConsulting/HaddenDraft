Crestview Heights Apartments respectfully requests that this Honorable Court grant possession of 145 Crestview Drive, Apt. 3B, to Plaintiff due to Defendant Jenkins's nonpayment of rent.

Statement of Facts
Crestview Heights Apartments is a project-based Section 8 housing project governed by 24 C.F.R. § 247.4. On February 10, 2023, Plaintiff served Defendant with a termination notice stating, 'Your tenancy will terminate on March 12, 2023.' The notice specified that 'the reason for this termination is nonpayment of rent: you owed $812.00 for February 2023, computed as of January 31, 2023.' It also stated, 'You have ten (10) days to discuss this termination with management.'

Argument
The termination notice complied with 24 C.F.R. § 247.4 in all respects.

The notice specified the termination date, stating 'Your tenancy will terminate on March 12, 2023,' thus meeting the regulation's requirements.

It provided concrete factual grounds, specifying 'you owed $812.00 for February 2023, computed as of January 31, 2023.'

The notice offered a ten-day opportunity to discuss termination, stating 'You have ten (10) days to discuss this termination with management.'

The notice was served on February 10, 2023, and the tenancy terminated on March 12, 2023, a period of thirty days, as required.

The arrearage was clearly identified as $812.00 for February 2023, computed as of January 31, 2023.

Conclusion
As the termination notice complied with 24 C.F.R. § 247.4, this Court should grant Plaintiff possession of 145 Crestview Drive, Apt. 3B.
