## Harrison v. Willow Creek

### Statement of Facts
Tenant Bryce Harrison has rented unit 3B at Willow Creek Apartments from Landlord Evergreen Properties since January 1, 2020. Rent is due on the first day of each month. On several occasions, Harrison has tendered rent late, and Evergreen Properties has accepted these late payments without objection. Specifically, Harrison paid the February 2023 rent on February 8, 2023, and the January 2023 rent on January 10, 2023. On March 7, 2023, Harrison tendered the March rent, which was accepted by Evergreen Properties' agent.

### Argument
The doctrine established in Finkbeiner v. Lutz guides this court's analysis. Under Finkbeiner, a landlord who has accepted late rent payments without objection may not evict a tenant for a late payment unless the landlord has given effective advance notice requiring strict future compliance. Here, Evergreen Properties accepted Harrison's late rent payments on multiple occasions.

The timing and frequency of prior late payments accepted by the landlord demonstrate an established practice of accepting rent tenders after the due date. Harrison's prior late payments were accepted by Evergreen Properties, establishing a pattern.

The disputed tender's date and amount complied with the established practice. Harrison tendered the March rent on March 7, 2023, which is consistent with the prior late payments accepted by Evergreen Properties.

### Conclusion
For the reasons stated above, this court should deny Evergreen Properties' request for eviction.
