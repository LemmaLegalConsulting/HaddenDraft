IN THE CUYAHOGA COUNTY COURT OF COMMON PLEAS

BURTON PROPERTIES LLC,
Plaintiff,
vs.
KAREN HAVENS,
Defendant.

MEMORANDUM IN SUPPORT OF DEFENDANT'S MOTION TO DISMISS

Statement of Facts

On June 1, 2022, the parties entered into a written lease agreement for the rental of 1456 East 105th Street, Apt. 3B, Cleveland, OH 44106. The monthly rent was $850, due on the 1st of each month with a 5-day grace period. On July 1, 2022, Havens timely tendered $850 via cashier's check No. 1234 drawn on PNC Bank. On July 6, 2022, Burton Properties served a 3-day notice to vacate, and subsequently filed this eviction action on July 10, 2022.

Argument

Ohio law provides that a landlord may not evict a tenant for nonpayment of rent when the tenant has tendered the full amount due. Pinewood Gardens Apts. v. Whiteside, 2014-Ohio-2305. Here, it is undisputed that Havens tendered the full July 2022 rent by the grace-period deadline.

The lease agreement and payment details confirm that Havens met her payment obligations.

The facts establish that Havens timely paid the rent in full, and Burton Properties' actions were not justified.

Conclusion

For the reasons stated, this court should dismiss the eviction action.
