IN THE CUYAHOGA COUNTY COURT

JOHN DOE, Plaintiff,
v.
JANE DOE, Defendant.

MEMORANDUM IN SUPPORT OF MOTION TO DISMISS

Statement of Facts
The landlord served a notice and later accepted a payment from the tenant.

Argument
The landlord's acceptance of a post-notice payment waived the notice under the rule in Associated Estates Corp. v. Bartell.

To establish waiver, we must show that the landlord served a notice, accepted payment after service, and that some accepted rent was attributable to a future period.

The payment covered the next month's occupancy, March, and was not just for past-due rent.

The notice was properly served, and payment was accepted afterward.

Conclusion
The court should dismiss this action or deny the landlord possession.
