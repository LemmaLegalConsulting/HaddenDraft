Jennings Property Management v. Jessica Wilson
Cuyahoga County Common Pleas Court

MEMORANDUM

Statement of Facts
This is an eviction action for alleged property damage. Jessica Wilson holds a Housing Choice Voucher issued under Section 8 tenant-based assistance. Jennings Property Management manages the rental property.

Argument

Wilson is protected under 34 U.S.C. § 12491 and 24 C.F.R. § 5.2005. The Housing Choice Voucher program is a covered housing program under 24 C.F.R. § 5.2003. Wilson is a victim of domestic violence. No documentation was requested, and Jennings Property Management has not asserted a VAWA exception.

Conclusion
The court should dismiss this eviction action or deny possession.
