MEMORANDUM IN SUPPORT OF RENT ESCROW
Statement of Facts
The tenant, Jane Doe, has been a resident at the dwelling in question since January 1, 2022. On January 5, 2023, she sent written notice to the landlord, John Smith, at the usual rent-payment location regarding the defective condition. The tenant was current with rent payments when she initiated this rent-escrow proceeding and deposited all rent due with the clerk on February 16, 2023. The dwelling is not student housing, and the landlord owns more than three rental units.

Argument
The landlord breached the duty to maintain the premises in a fit and habitable condition under R.C. 5321.04. The specific obligation breached was the duty to keep the premises in a fit and habitable condition.

The tenant has complied with R.C. 5321.07(A) by providing written notice to the landlord at the usual rent-payment location. As the landlord failed to remedy the defect, the tenant is entitled to an order reducing rent under R.C. 5321.07(B)(2). The entry claim is inapplicable as no entry claim is asserted under R.C. 5321.04(A)(8) or (B), so elements related to entry are not applicable. The consequence claimed is rent reduction, as expressly provided by R.C. 5321.07(B)(2).

Conclusion
The tenant respectfully requests that this court grant an order reducing rent as per R.C. 5321.07(B)(2).
