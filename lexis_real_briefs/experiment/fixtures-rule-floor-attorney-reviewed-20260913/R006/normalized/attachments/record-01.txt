AFFIDAVIT OF JANE DOE
I, Jane Doe, being duly sworn, state that on January 1, 2023, the furnace in the basement of the dwelling ceased functioning, resulting in the living room being without heat. I sent written notice to John Smith at the usual rent-payment location on January 5, 2023. The furnace remained unrepaired until February 15, 2023. The dwelling is not student housing, and John Smith owns more than three rental units.

Sworn and subscribed before me this 20th day of February, 2023.
