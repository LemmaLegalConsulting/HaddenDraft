IN THE COURT OF COMMON PLEAS
Cuyahoga County, Ohio

JENNY TENANT,
Plaintiff,

v.

MARK LANDLORD,
Defendant.

CASE NO. 2023 CV 000001

MEMORANDUM IN SUPPORT OF DAMAGES FOR UNLAWFUL LOCKOUT

STATEMENT OF FACTS
The plaintiff, Jenny Tenant, rented a residential property from Mark Landlord. On January 10, 2023, Landlord locked Tenant out without a court order. Tenant had not abandoned the premises.

ARGUMENT
R.C. 5321.15 prohibits a landlord from recovering possession of residential premises except through judicial process. Here, the landlord-tenant relationship is established by the rental agreement between Jenny Tenant and Mark Landlord, satisfying the first element.
The second element is met because Mark Landlord's lockout without a court order constitutes an unlawful act to recover possession.

The lockout caused Jenny Tenant to incur $250 in hotel costs and $100 for a locksmith to regain entry, demonstrating actual harm resulting from Landlord's prohibited act.

R.C. 5321.15(C) allows the tenant to recover actual damages caused by the violation and reasonable attorney fees. The court should rule in Tenant's favor on this doctrine.

CONCLUSION
The court should award Jenny Tenant actual damages and reasonable attorney fees.
