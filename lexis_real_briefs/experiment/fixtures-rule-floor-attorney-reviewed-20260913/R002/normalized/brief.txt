MEMORANDUM IN OPPOSITION TO EVICTION

Statement of Facts
The landlord alleges a breach of R.C. 5321.05 materially affecting health and safety.

Argument
R.C. 5321.11 governs termination for such breaches.

The written notice did not specify the noncompliant act or omission as required by R.C. 5321.11, quoting only a generic 'lease violation'.

The written notice was delivered and received on February 1, 2023.
The termination date was March 15, 2023, more than thirty days after receipt.
A March 5 inspection showed the condition remained after the February 1 receipt and thirty-day cure period.
Trash blocking the common fire-exit route violated R.C. 5321.05 and materially affected fire safety.
The condition was not remedied within the statutory period.

Conclusion
The court is respectfully requested to dismiss this action or deny the landlord possession.
