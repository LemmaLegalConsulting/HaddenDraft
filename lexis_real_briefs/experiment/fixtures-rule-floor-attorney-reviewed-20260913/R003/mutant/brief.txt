IN THE COURT OF COMMON PLEAS
Cuyahoga County, Ohio

JOHN DOE, Plaintiff,
v.
JANE DOE, Defendant.

CASE NO. 2023 CV 000001

MEMORANDUM IN SUPPORT OF POSSESSION

Statement of Facts
The plaintiff-landlord served the defendant-tenant with a written notice specifying a covered breach and a termination date.

Argument
The landlord is entitled to possession under R.C. 5321.11.

The written notice was properly delivered and received, specifying the noncompliant act and a termination date more than thirty days after receipt.
The covered breach involves an obligation under R.C. 5321.05.

Conclusion
The court should grant possession to the plaintiff.
