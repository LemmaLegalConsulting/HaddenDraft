150.52 Reasonable fees for late payment of rent.
If a rental agreement includes a provision that authorizes the landlord to assess the tenant a fee for late payment of the monthly rent, the total amount of that late payment fee for any month may not exceed the larger of: (i) twenty-five dollars; or (ii) eight percent of the monthly contract rent.
(Ord. 111-2021)
